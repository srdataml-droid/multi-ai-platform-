## Decisions and findings that must not be lost

1. The existing HVAC pack has **90/60/45-minute demo values**, respectively for `repair_visit`, `boiler_service`, `estimate`. These are sample configuration, not business-approved evidence. The reviewed scheduler's unknown/missing-duration fallback is 60 minutes; “other” maps to repair automatically. Both are recorded implementation gaps, not fixed defects.
2. Proposed scopes preserve those three codes: heating fault assessment, routine boiler service and heating replacement survey. Assessment does not guarantee a same-visit repair; a survey does not book an installation. Landlord gas safety checks, heat-pump maintenance and return/remedial work need separately enabled scopes.
3. All six prepared profiles are disabled drafts. Approved minutes, fees, competence/crew and business policy remain empty. The catalogue JSON is a review format, **not a supported runtime import**.
4. Booking requires applicable scope and duration evidence, qualified available resources, confirmed address, both route legs, protected time and current sources. Recheck before confirmation. A visible calendar gap or staff approval does not establish these facts.
5. Approval, external booking and customer notification are separate outcomes. Reconcile uncertain provider results before retrying. A booked visit whose message failed retries the message without creating another booking. Never claim an escalation was delivered without its actual result.
6. Approval-policy wording needs reconciliation: some documentation/prompts imply broad office approval; the reviewed gate allows configured low-risk actions and rejects high-risk actions. Resolve the actual policy before changing executors or demonstrating promises.
7. API-first plus retrieval/owned tools is the starting recommendation. Benchmark an existing small model on the same held-out tasks later; fine-tuning requires a demonstrated narrow benefit, data rights/privacy evidence, economics and rollback. Existing repository training restrictions remain in force until deliberately reconciled.
8. The suggested target segment (small teams, provisionally 2–15 staff), £249/month example, setup pricing and contribution estimates are **research hypotheses**, not founder-approved customer prices or measured profit. Vendor prices/terms and UK outreach rules are dated research to recheck before action.
9. The shorthand “tg” was not resolved into a Telegram request. Do not create that integration based on the shorthand alone.
