How the test works
  1. Before connection: this company chat uses your configured model for general replies. It cannot book or create inbox records. Sign in to Novaxis in this browser first.
  2. Connect: enter your Novaxis business slug and choose Connect. No API key or password belongs here. General chat requires your signed-in Novaxis owner session; connected visitor chat does not.
  3. After connection: messages go to that business’s Novaxis inbox. Its assistant and approval rules handle the request.
  4. Verify: check Inbox, Approvals and Schedule in Novaxis. A request is not a confirmed booking.
